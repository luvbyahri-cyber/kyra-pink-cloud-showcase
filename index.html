Kyra’s
Pink Cloud

A digital-products storefront with a buyer catalog, GCash receipt review, and a dashboard for managing orders and delivery.

Explore the order flow

From browsing to delivery

  1. Choose a product

    Buyers browse the catalog by category, choose a product variant, and place an order.

  2. Submit a receipt

    Buyers upload a GCash receipt. Staff review it from the order dashboard and can request details in the order conversation.

  3. Deliver the order

    After payment approval, staff can deliver from reserved stock or enter delivery details manually. Buyers see the details in My Orders.

How it’s built

React + Vite
Buyer storefront and admin dashboard
Express
API, cookie-based sign-in, and receipt uploads
SQLite
Catalog, inventory, and order records
